Hinata

Hinata is not a todo app.

Your life runs on tracks: work, health, the people you care about, the things you're learning. Hinata keeps a record of the small steps you take on each of them.

How it works

  • Capture a thought the moment you have it. It waits in staging until you decide what to do with it.
  • Commit it as a step for today, on one track.
  • Take the step with a press and hold. Not taken by midnight, it's missed.
  • Taken and missed steps stay in your record for good. Over time, each track shows the path you actually walked.

Private by design. Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, no internet access, no analytics and no ads.

Availability

Hinata is in early testing on Android. It's coming to Google Play.
