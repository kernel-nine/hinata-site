Hinata

Hinata is not a todo app.

Your life runs on tracks: work, health, the people you care about, the things you're learning. Hinata keeps a record of the small steps you take on each of them.

How it works

  • Capture a thought the moment you have it. It waits in staging until you decide what to do with it.
  • Commit it as a step for today, on one track.
  • Take the step with a press and hold. Not taken by midnight, it's missed.
  • Taken and missed steps stay in your record for good. Over time, each track shows the path you actually walked.

Private by design. Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, no internet access, no analytics and no ads.

Download

Hinata is in early testing (alpha) on Android 7 and later. It's coming to Google Play; until then, install it directly:

Download for Android

  1. Open the downloaded file. When asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. On Samsung phones, turn off Auto Blocker first (Settings › Security and privacy). You can turn it back on afterwards.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, choose "Install anyway".
  4. For the evening nudge to arrive on time, set Hinata's battery use to "Unrestricted".

Alpha means rough edges, and there's no export yet: uninstalling deletes your data. Every release is signed with the same key, certificate SHA-256 8cd6b997c977c074afadb6a756760ba99a2e67926cefa40736ec75f84132d6b9. Checksums and changes for each version are on the releases page.
